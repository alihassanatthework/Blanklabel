## Overview

This policy explains what information BlankLabels.com collects, how we use it and the choices you have. This page is part of a website demo; the final policy will be provided by BlankLabels.com before launch.

## Information we collect

- **Order details** - name, shipping address, email and phone so we can deliver your order.
- **Account details** - saved printers, addresses and order history if you create an account.
- **Messages** - anything you send through our contact, quote or sample forms.

## How we use it

We use your information to process orders, answer questions, send order updates and, if you subscribe, send offers. We never sell your personal information.

## Payment information

Card payments are processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
